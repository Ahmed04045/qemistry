Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 12, 2026

Welcome to the Qemistry Beta. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our website and video chat platform. By using Qemistry, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

To operate our platform, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Username, email address, date of birth (optional), and user profile details (such as bios, interests, and profile pictures).
  • Technical & Connection Data: We log your IP address, which is strictly essential to establish connections to our video servers.
  • Camera & Microphone Access: We request access to your device's camera and microphone exclusively when you are actively broadcasting on the platform. We do not record, monitor, or store your live video feeds.
  • Communications: Text chat messages, user reports, and friend lists.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to provide, maintain, and improve the Qemistry platform. This includes authenticating your account, facilitating live video connections, displaying your profile to other users, and enforcing our 18+ age restriction and Terms & Conditions.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

If you choose to sign up or log in using Google OAuth, we adhere strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy. Here is exactly how we handle your Google data:

  • Data Accessed: Our application requests access to your primary Google email address, your name, and your profile picture.
  • Data Usage: This data is used strictly for account creation, seamless authentication into the Qemistry platform, and populating your public Qemistry profile.
  • Data Sharing: Qemistry does not share, sell, distribute, or transfer your Google user data to any third parties, ad networks, or external services.
  • Data Storage & Protection: Your Google authentication data is stored securely using industry-standard database security protocols on our primary servers located in Germany.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: Your Google-related data is retained only for as long as your Qemistry account remains active. You can request the complete deletion of your account and all associated Google data at any time by contacting us at qemistry.live@gmail.com.

4. General Data Retention & Deletion

We practice aggressive data minimization to protect our users:

  • Ephemeral Chat Logs: All text chats on Qemistry are temporary. Our systems automatically and permanently delete all chat messages 24 hours after they are sent.
  • Account Deletion: You hold the right to delete your account. Upon requesting deletion, your profile, friend lists, and associated personal data will be wiped from our active databases.

5. Third-Party Advertisements

Qemistry displays third-party advertisements (such as through Adsterra). Clicking these ads directs you to external websites. We do not share your personal account details or Google user data with these ad networks. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

6. Age Policy (18+)

Qemistry is strictly for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we discover that a user is under 18, their account and all associated data will be immediately and permanently deleted.
